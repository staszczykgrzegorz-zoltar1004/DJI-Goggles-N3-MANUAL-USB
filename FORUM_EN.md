Title: **N3 Manual — free Android USB menu remote for DJI Goggles N3, Neo and FPV RC3 (MIT)**

Hi everyone,

I am sharing N3 Manual, a small free Android application that operates the DJI
Goggles N3 menu over USB and helps restore the Custom Mode assignment to Manual
with the original DJI Neo and DJI FPV Remote Controller 3. I developed it as a
workaround for a damaged physical 5D button and a lost Manual assignment after
switching devices.

It reads the menu, navigates to Manual, and lets you confirm after looking
inside the goggles. Manual arrow buttons and OK/Back are also available.
It does not modify firmware, permanently freeze settings, disable Normal/Sport,
or arm the aircraft. Use it on the bench, with propellers removed and motors off.

Version 1.0.0-beta2 supports English and Polish, automatically follows the phone
language (Polish for Polish, English otherwise), and includes a manual language
picker. Android 6.0+, no root, account, ads or Internet permission. The interface,
help, progress messages and errors are translated. This is an independent tool,
not affiliated with DJI.

Earlier prototype hardware feedback is from a Hammer Construction 2 Thermal 5G
with Android 14 and the N3/Neo/RC3 set. Emulator tests cover Android app
compatibility, not physical USB on every phone. See the current test report
for the exact public APK and limits.
